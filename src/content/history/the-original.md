Whispers in Akarra was a free 2D online role-playing game that ran from April 2002 to February 2004. It was created by Jens "Khaile" Bergensten as a hobby project, and grew to more than 2,000 accounts.

## The game

Jens began work on the game in the summer of 2001, and the first test version went online on April 28, 2002. New versions followed every few weeks, adding levels, monsters, items, trading and sound, and a small team of volunteers joined in to build the world, draw the artwork and run events.

Pre-Alpha 12, in October 2002, brought new artwork by Kinten and thewreck along with shopkeepers and quests. Pre-Alpha 13, in spring 2003, added spells and classes. Alpha 1 followed in June 2003 and opened the game's busiest summer, with around 100 players online at once, thirty clans, story events in the town of Hope and even an in-game wedding.

The server was switched off on February 14, 2004. Jens later released the server program and the source code, and players have run their own servers, called [shards](/history/shards), ever since.

## After Akarra

Jens founded Oxeye Game Studio in 2003, where he worked with Akarra's two artists, and the studio went on to make The Strategist, Harvest and Cobalt. He joined Mojang in 2010, became lead designer and lead developer of Minecraft in 2011, and still directs the game today as Mojang's chief creative officer.

A little of Akarra lives on in Minecraft. The splash texts "Bekarton guards the gate!" and "Big Pointy Teeth!" refer to an Akarra gatekeeper and the game's rabbits, and the Wither and the Blaze are both named after Akarra spells.

## Key dates

- **April 28, 2002:** The first pre-alpha goes online.
- **October 27, 2002:** Pre-Alpha 12.
- **June 18, 2003:** Alpha 1.
- **February 14, 2004:** The server is switched off.
- **July 2005:** The server program is released, and the first [shards](/history/shards) open.
- **December 2011:** Jens becomes lead designer and lead developer of Minecraft.
