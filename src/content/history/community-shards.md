A "shard" is a fan-run server for Whispers in Akarra. Players have been running them since 2005, and two are online today: see [Play Today](/community/play) to join one.

## How shards began

In July 2005 Jens Bergensten released the game's server program and its editors, which let players host the game themselves and build new maps, monsters, items and quests. In February 2008 he published the source code as well, so shards could change the game itself.

## Shards

In the order they opened.

- **Project Phoenix** (2005–2006): One of the first shards, run by Origon, Braiba and Talonz, who maintains this site, with its own client.
- **Akarrian Soul** (2005–2006): Aquarits's shard from Brazil, with a new campaign, "Legacy of the Dragon", and new maps and sprites.
- **Akarrian Expansion** (2005–2010): Apocalypse's shard, also called AkarraXP, which added new classes, skills, spells, sectors and guild houses. Its files are the basis of several later shards, including Akarra X2.
- **Emperium Xenos** (2005–2010): Founded by Gorgorath, tbbw, Luna and Nazzy, with its own client and custom interfaces, and decisions made by a vote of its developers.
- **Akarra 2** (2006–2007): A continuation of the game built on its full source code, presented as "Whispers in Akarra II" with a story set fifteen years later. It released two alpha versions in 2007.
- **Akarrian Rebirth** (2010–2011): A shard with every class unlocked.
- **Akarra: Millennium Edition** (2011): A short-lived project started by ssdsp with a volunteer team.
- **Classic Akarra** (2011–2015): Kingmarr's plain Akarra server, run while new content was developed behind the scenes.
- **Gorgorath's server** (2012–2013): A public server set up by Gorgorath, one of Emperium Xenos's founders.
- **iAkarra** (2013–2015): A classic Alpha 1 server with small changes, run by two brothers who planned to merge the best of the older shards into it.
- **Mostly Vanilla** (2014 onward, currently offline): PT6's "nearly vanilla" copy of the original game, first hosted "for nostalgic purposes" and published in 2022.
- **AkarraXP** (2015): A revival of Apocalypse's Akarrian Expansion, with an updated client and new item and crafting lists.
- **AkarraXP Reborn** (2015–2016): BudMan's rebalanced and revamped continuation of the 2015 revival.
- **AkarraXP Classic** (2016): Spaz's continuation of the 2015 revival, which kept everyone's existing characters.
- **Akarra X2** (2018 to today): Apocalypse's Akarrian Expansion with double experience and rebalanced classes, played in the browser since September 2026.
- **Whispers in Akarra X3** (2023–2025): Dakota's one-person expansion of Apocalypse's server, with new classes, islands and guilds up to level 200.
- **Akarra Online** (2026 to today): Steven's rebuild of the game on a new engine, played in the browser and on phones. Besides its main shard, it hosts older versions of the game side by side, including the original world, a hardcore version and Apocalypse's Akarrian Expansion.
