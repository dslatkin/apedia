Long time, no update! Only 4 years this time. Better than the previous gap of 17 years, right? :D

Anyways, I'm excited to announce a major update to the Akarrian Encyclopedia. The site has been rebuilt using modern web technology. Additionally, several pages have been added, updated, and reorganized to align with the current state of the game.

I tried to keep the design true to the original, so the changes might be a bit hard to see at first glance, but in general they include:

- Responsive design and a modern nav menu that now works on mobile.
- Reorganization of pages and new and updated content for modern-day Akarra.
- Many other smaller improvements sprinkled across many pages and experiences.

This rebuild was in large part motivated by a burst of recent activity in the community following the launch of Steven's "Akarra Online", the first project making big use of AI to bring the game to modern platforms with many new features. Shortly after, "Akarra X2", running since 2018, also became playable in the browser. Check out both active projects on the new [play today page](/community/play).

If you run a shard or see any information worth fixing or updating, please use the [contact me page](/contact) which has a new form and updated contact methods... Or if you'd just like to get in touch and say hi!

Until next time.
